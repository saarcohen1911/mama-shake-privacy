Mama Shake privacy policy

Mama Shake is a free browser game. This page explains what information the game uses.

Leaderboard

If you save a score, the game stores the name you type, the country you pick, your best score and a random player ID kept in your browser. This is shown publicly on the leaderboard. Don't use your real full name if you don't want it public.

On your device

The game keeps your language, best score, name and player ID in your browser's local storage so they're remembered next time. You can clear them by clearing your browser data.

Advertising

The web version of the game may show ads from Google. The iPhone app does not currently show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and measure them, based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more and manage ad personalization at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and adssettings.google.com.
